# Review: REPLACE_ROLE

## Scope and traceability
Reviewer identity, requirement IDs/files inspected, commit, input paths/hashes.

## Existing facts versus proposals
Repository facts and approved requirements separately from new recommendations.

## Findings
Finding ID, severity, confirmed/risk/question, location, impact and action;
or explicitly no findings.

## Sources and evidence
Verified sources, exact supported claims, populations and applicability;
or evidence not verified with the available tools.

## Confidence and limitations
Confidence with reasons, missing inputs, tool/platform limits and self-review.

## Acceptance criteria and edge cases
Existing criteria/test traceability; separately proposed criteria and boundaries.

## Dependencies
Role slugs, interfaces, tickets and specific cross-domain challenge questions.

## Safety concerns
Harm, contraindications, referrals, privacy or none found (not clinical clearance).

## Unresolved product decisions
Questions, alternatives, impact and actual decision authority.

## Verification
Actual results supplied/inspected versus recommended tests; no invented runs.

## Targeted challenges
Append labeled follow-ups; preserve the initial independent report.
