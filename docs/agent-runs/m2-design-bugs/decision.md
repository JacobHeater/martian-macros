# Panel decision: the M2 design bugs, fixed in one batch

- **Facts versus proposals and traceability.** The owner asked (2026-10-19) for the beta's tickets to be closed on one branch. Seven design bugs the panel's earlier runs had found were fixed on `feat/m2` and reviewed together, the stories that draw them already existing. Two read-only reviews of the working tree (baseline `main` 39f315c): the product designer and the UX guard, each from the brief and the stories read from source (no browser on the owner's Mac in this session). The chair (the parent session) edited, ran the tests and wrote this record.
- **Verdicts.** UX guard: **pass, no blocking finding**, one high (F1). Product designer: nine findings, three medium.
- **Consolidated coverage.** Inspected: the components, stylesheet, tokens, stories, tests and tickets of all seven fixes, every `Notice` and `StatRow` call site, the direction documents. Not inspected by anyone: a render in any palette, a device, assistive technology, the engine's numbers.
- **Findings and disposition** (deduplicated by cause):
  - *The status region is inserted with its text, so the announcement is AT-dependent* (F1 high): **accepted.** The scan step renders a `role="status"` container from first paint and places the notice inside it; `Notice` no longer carries a live role of its own. `status` kept over `alert` (ARIA 1.2), as the guard confirmed. The regression test checks the empty region before the lookup. The device check (TalkBack, VoiceOver) is on the owner's list.
  - *The camera-unavailable path drops focus and announces nothing* (F2 medium): **a bug ticket, MM-413**, proposed, with its skipped guard; not fixed silently in this batch, as the guard asked.
  - *The hairline on every notice: undocumented, and wrong inside a sheet well* (PD-1, F5): **accepted.** The hairline holds on the canvas; an info notice drawn as a well of a sheet or dialog has none; `design/screen-direction.md`'s notice table says so; the rule test covers both.
  - *The hero lines are a new pattern with no named sibling* (PD-3 medium) and *"Alcohol: 109 kcal" does not say how it is counted* (F9): **accepted.** The lines are rows shaped like the macro rows' heads (`HeroFactLine`: name at the start, figures at the end), and the alcohol row says "109 kcal, in the total". The chair's answer to both reviewers' question: the hero's figure sums every entry's kcal (`intakeDayFrom.ts`), alcohol included (`FoodEntry.alcoholKcal` is derived from the same entry), and only the Split picture leaves it out, as the disclosure under the meals says.
  - *The two-line hero state is drawn by no story* (PD-4 medium): **accepted.** `FoodWithAlcohol` saves Full detail, so the fiber row shows beside the alcohol row; the brief's `FoodFullDetail` name was wrong.
  - *Two patterns for what a figure rests on, two sections apart* (PD-5 medium): **accepted.** `StatRow`'s `detail` is the one home, in the caption role; the pace detail moved onto it.
  - *The ticket's full detail is undrawn; words remain in sibling values* (PD-6): **accepted.** Story `CoachFewDaysEstimated` draws "against a 2,100 kcal target: 250 over · rests on 3 days · mostly estimated"; "Days logged" is "7" with "6 complete" under it.
  - *"against 2,100" names no noun* (F10): **accepted**, the chair deciding for the coaching role: the target is a fact of MM-149's summary, never a score, so naming it is within its rule: "against a 2,100 kcal target".
  - *At 200% the stat row with a detail loses its figure column* (F3): **accepted.** The value keeps the end of its line (`margin-inline-start: auto`); story `CoachLargeText`.
  - *The title breaks inside a word without hyphenation* (F7) and *the action can take the whole row before the words* (F8): **accepted.** `hyphens: auto` on the title; the action at most half the row.
  - *Two empty rows carry icons by different logic* (PD-7): **accepted**, `info` for "Nothing downloaded yet".
  - *Corner radius differs from the groups* (PD-2): **the owner's word** (handoff); 12 px kept meanwhile, `.mm-surface-compact` being the precedent.
  - *A sibling announces by a different means* (PD-9): **declined.** The pack card's persistent `role="status"` wrapper is the robust pattern F1 asks for; the `live` prop was the fragile one and is withdrawn.
  - *The hairline's contrast* (F6): no change required; informational. *One stop or three for a stat row* (F4): device check, owner's list. *Mid-word breaks in the labels* (PD-8): the owner's look at the 200% stories.
- **Targeted challenges.** None run: the reports agree where they overlap (PD-1/F5, PD-3/F9, PD-5/F3) and differ on nothing material.
- **Safety and privacy.** Neither reviewer found a concern; the Coach summary keeps MM-149's facts-only wording.
- **Owner decisions.** PD-2 (the notice's radius); the renders by eye in the three palettes: `FoodWithAlcohol`, `CoachStalledOverTarget`, `CoachFewDaysEstimated`, `CoachLargeText`, `ScanUnknownCode`, `FoodDatabase*`, `FoodLargeText`, `DashboardLargeText`; the device checks for F1 and F4.
