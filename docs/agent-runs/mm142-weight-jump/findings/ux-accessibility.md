## Existing facts versus proposals
Facts (cited in the report's walkthrough): the explanation is the whole `weightJumpText` sentence as the closed line of an `InlineInsight layout="row"` under the trend row, figures as `StatRow`s one tap down, then "Settings for “A jump on the scale”"; no Snooze. Withheld under 18, with an eating-disorder history or with weight hidden; not governed by the insight switches. `itemInsightKinds.ts` has no kind titled "A jump on the scale" (nor "Leaving a deficit"); `CoachAreaScreen` focuses a row only when a kind's title matches. The trend chart's `<svg role="img">` takes `onClick`; a tap on a dot or within 12 chart units picks it; no `tabIndex`, key handler or alternative control. Without a pick, only the latest reading in range is explained, and only when far. The waist protocol is an SVG figure (`role="img"`, one-sentence label) beside a list of four captions, in full while `entries < 3`, then under "How to measure". `WaistChart` draws dots, a line through the middle of up to three neighbours and a band from two smoothed points. Type tokens are rem-based; nothing sets `textZoom` or `-apple-system-body`.
Proposals are the findings below; none is promoted to a requirement.

## Novice mobile experience (when UX/product design is assigned)
**Goal A:** "I weighed 1.3 kg more than yesterday; is it real?" Journey: Today, the weigh-in field, saved, the trend row, the coach's line, the figures, (Settings). Notice: the Ion line under the row is about the number above it. Decide: nothing; no choice is needed to get the answer. Interpret: "trend" as the app's own number, "day-to-day noise" as the threshold, and the arithmetic (a kcal figure against "expenditure"). Competing actions: the row (opens Progress) and the line's toggle, two adjacent full-width buttons, as for the rebound sibling. Routine versus uncertainty: the "likely" framing and the "nothing in your log" honesty sit in the closed line; the arithmetic is education. Recovery: back from the figures is the same toggle; from the dead-end Settings screen, the system Back. Route to detail: one tap, then one more to Settings (which fails, UX-1).
**Goal B:** "Measure my waist the way the app wants, and see where it is going." Journey: Progress, past the hero and events, the Waist header, open, the drawing and steps, three fields, Save, the chart. Decide: one reading or three (the cost of one is said only after typing one, the right moment); 90d or 1y. Interpret: "the middle one is kept"; a flat line through neither of two dots (UX-7). Recovery: Save stays quiet until the entry is valid; a wrong value gets a snack. Detail: the drawing in full until the third measurement, then one tap down with its key line still in the subtitle.
Burdens evidenced by the step records: UX-1, UX-2, UX-3, UX-5, UX-7. Hypotheses (unobserved): UX-3b, UX-12. Card count played no part.

## Findings
- **UX-1** (coach-insights / react-capacitor; reproducible implementation defect by source; **high**, confidence high). `InlineInsight.tsx:93-98` always draws the Settings button; `weightJumpContent.ts:15` names "A jump on the scale"; `itemInsightKinds.ts` has no such kind; `CoachAreaScreen.tsx:46, 87` names and focuses nothing; `useWeightJump.ts:36` ignores the switches, so neither "Body insights" nor "all insights off" quiets the line. Expected: the button opens this line's setting, or there is no button. The sibling "Leaving a deficit" (`DashboardScreen.tsx`) has the same gap, pre-existing; its own bug ticket. Remedies: (a) an `ItemInsightKind` for the jump honouring the switches; or (b) the Settings button only when a kind exists, and MM-142 says the line has no switch. Acceptance: pressing the button lands on a row of that name with its switch focused, or the button is absent.
- **UX-2** (coach-insights / product-designer; usability problem by inspection; **medium**, confidence medium). `weightJumpText.ts:33-79` joins head, causes, the low-reading sentence and the arithmetic or overshoot sentence into one closed line: 228 characters in the walked case, about 287 for `WeighInOvershoot`; 5-6 caption lines at 100%, 9-11 at 200%, under a two-line row. Proposal (owner and coaching role): keep head, causes, the honesty sentences in the line; move "For this to be fat…" to the figures only; keep the overshoot sentence. MM-142 "carries the arithmetic line once" is where a change would be made. Acceptance: a 200% story on 412 px in the three palettes; the closed line no more than about one line longer than the row in the common case.
- **UX-3** (progress / accessibility; usability problem by inspection; **medium**, confidence high on mechanism, low on impact). WCAG 2.2 SC 2.1.1: `TrendChart.tsx:101-107` is pointer-only; the no-pointer path covers only the latest reading, and only when far. Remedies: a "Recent weigh-ins" disclosure listing the range's readings as rows; or `tabIndex=0` on the pickable chart with ArrowLeft/ArrowRight moving `pickedDay` and the caption as the announced result. Acceptance: with no pointer, any in-range weigh-in's value and trend distance can be reached; a keyboard test. **UX-3b** (unverified hypothesis, low): a TalkBack/VoiceOver double-tap dispatches a click at the element's centre, which `pick` may read as the nearest dot; device check.
- **UX-4** (progress; discoverability; low, confidence medium). No sign that dots are tappable; a 5.7 px dot is not plainly pressable. Proposal: one clause in "How to read this": "Tap a dot to see that weigh-in."
- **UX-5** (progress; redundancy; low, confidence high). `WeighInNote.tsx:28-39` with `weightJumpText.ts:26-31`: picked plus far from trend says the distance twice. Proposal: with an explanation, the caption says only the day and reading.
- **UX-6** (coach-insights; improvement; low, confidence high). "add it as a weight event in Progress" points a person on Progress to the screen they are on; on the Dashboard the route is three steps. Proposal: a Progress-specific suffix naming the section.
- **UX-7** (body-measurements / charts; usability problem by inspection; **low-medium**, confidence high on geometry). `WaistChart.tsx:28-33, 52-57, 73, 93`: with two entries the line is flat at their mean and touches neither dot; with one, `linePath` yields a bare `M` and the band is omitted while the caption describes a line and a band. Proposal: line and band from three entries; below that dots with a caption that says so. Acceptance: `ProgressWaistFirstMeasurements` and a one-measurement story read true; tests for one and two entries.
- **UX-8** (progress; improvement; low). SC 2.5.8: the active column is about 27 px but neighbours overlap at daily weigh-ins; not a violation under the dense-data exception; the keyboard path in UX-3 is the alternative. No further change.
- **UX-9** (body-measurements; documentation gap; low, confidence high). A person who measures from the Dashboard (`WaistRow`, one field) never sees the drawing; by the time they open Progress it may be folded. Proposal: say in MM-155 the drawing belongs to the Progress entry, or show "How to measure" from the Dashboard row. Owner's call.
- **UX-10** (ui / accessibility; improvement; low). `WaistProtocolFigure.tsx:17`: the alternative restates steps 2 and 3, read immediately after. Option: say what the drawing adds ("Drawing: where the tape sits, level around the waist at the navel.").
- **UX-11** (react-capacitor; informational). `progress.css` `.progress-waist-protocol-folded` is used nowhere.
- **UX-12** (cross-cutting; unverified hypothesis, low). Rem tokens follow the root size; WKWebView may not scale rem with Dynamic Type; nothing sets `textZoom` or `-apple-system-body`. If true, every 200% statement holds for Android only. Pre-existing; bounded request: check text scale on the iOS build.
- Contrast (computed from tokens): the Ion caption on the surface 11.9:1 (Martian), 10.6:1 (Dark), 5.1:1 (Light; AA for 12 px); the figure's `--text2` strokes on white 7.8:1; the picked dot is distinguished by size and words as well as colour.
- Surveyed notes (not findings): `waistSummary.ts:23` drops the due clause once the week has passed; the waist chart's colour is `--carbs` via `.chart-measurement` (pre-existing; comprehension question for the designer); the report's "No clear change (…)" is long for a `StatRow` value at 200% but uses the app's phrase.

## Acceptance criteria and edge cases
Existing criteria trace to `weightJump.test.tsx` and `weightJumpChart.test.tsx`; "Several causes" with a 10 pm meal is a stated non-goal; "Hidden numbers" rests on a flag MM-118 has not built; MM-155's drawing, fold and chart trace to `waistProtocol.test.tsx`; the chart's one- and two-entry pictures have no test. Proposed: the Settings route lands on a named, focused row or the button is absent; 200% stories; a non-pointer route to any in-range reading; one- and two-entry waist chart stories and tests. Edge cases: imperial captions (Unicode minus); a picked day whose weigh-in is later deleted (nothing drawn: fine); an imported weigh-in counts as weighed today; a far-below reading for a person with the ED flag is withheld entirely (correct, MM-141).
