# Panel decision: the nights of sleep on Progress and the sleep line on Coach (MM-250, MM-252)

- **Facts versus proposals and traceability.** The owner decided on 2026-10-13 that sleep is first class (MM-249); MM-250 builds the record and the typed card, MM-252 the coach's reading. Two read-only reviews of the working tree of `wt/sleep` (baseline `feat/m2` 24130ec, the tree later committed as c5ac538): the product designer and the UX guard, each given the brief and the stories as the render, read from source; no browser, device or assistive technology existed in this session. The chair (the sleep workstream session) edited, ran the tests and wrote this record.
- **Verdicts.** UX guard: **pass, no blocking finding**; F1 high, confirmed by running the tests it named (two check-in tests failed on the anchored names). Product designer: thirteen findings, one high (PD-1), six medium.
- **Consolidated coverage.** Inspected by both: the Sleep section, `MmDurationField`, the recovery section's sleep line, the offer, the check-in pre-fill, their siblings, the design-system parts, the CSS, the eight stories, the three screen tests, the two tickets and the epic, the direction. Not inspected by anyone: a render in any palette, a phone, TalkBack or VoiceOver, text at 200%, the Settings health rows, the health step beyond its source, the engine thresholds, the native plugins, the schema.
- **Findings and disposition** (deduplicated by cause):
  - *The two fields are heard with the label twice, and the anchored queries in a story and two tests do not match* (F1 high): **accepted, verified first.** `npx vitest run test/coach/recoveryCheckIn.test.tsx test/ui/fieldErrors.test.tsx` showed the two check-in tests failing as predicted. `MmDurationField` now names its fields "<night>, Hours" and "<night>, Minutes", which contain the label, so the name alone is heard; the tests assert the exact name.
  - *A minutes error marks the hours field* (F3, PD-6 medium): **accepted as a bug, MM-414**, fixed with its regression test: `MmDurationField.error` names the field it concerns; `parseDurationFields` decides which. The Fields catalog and a story (`SleepMinutesMistyped`) show the state.
  - *A fractional average pre-fills "60" minutes* (F5 medium): **accepted as a bug, MM-415**, fixed with its test: one helper, `durationFieldsOf`, rounds to the minute before splitting, used by the check-in and the card in place of two copies.
  - *A loading or failed read renders nothing on Progress and reads "Not recorded" on Coach, with no story* (F4, PD-8 medium): **accepted.** The section shows `ReadFailedNotice` with Try again (`AppState.retrySleep`) and a spinner while loading, as the strength section does; the Coach line says "Not read" with where to try again; stories `SleepReadFailed`, `SleepLoading`, `CoachSleepReadFailed`; tests on both screens.
  - *Saving gives no sign where the action was taken or aloud* (F2 medium, PD-13 low): **accepted.** A polite snack "Saved: Last night, 7 h 20 m." through the status host already used for failure; the focus blur stays, as every entry on the app does to dismiss the keyboard (MM-317's rule), noted for the guard.
  - *Nothing beside the fields names the night being typed* (PD-4 medium, F10 low): **accepted** by the note: it leads with the picked night in every state ("The night before: not recorded yet. 3 of the last 14 nights recorded."), beside Save. No visible label was added to the field, so the heard name does not double.
  - *The Save's placement differs from Waist without a reason and likely misaligns* (PD-2 medium, F9): **accepted, by adopting the sibling's layout**: the fields in their row, then the note beside a quiet Save beneath, as `WaistEntryCard` draws it; the brief's description of the sibling was wrong and is corrected here. Alignment at 200% text still needs a render (Q4).
  - *Two invalid states disable Save without a word* (F6 low) and *the check-in drops an out-of-range value silently* (F7, PD-7): **accepted.** "Up to 24 hours in all." and "Type the hours asleep." join the two messages; the check-in uses the same `parseDurationFields`, says the error under the field, holds Save while it stands, and its caption changes once the person has typed. Story `RecoveryCheckInMistyped`; test "more than a day of sleep is refused under the field and holds Save".
  - *The fields and Save sit bare on the canvas inside the open section* (PD-1 high): **the owner's call, as a proposed ticket, MM-416.** The Waist section has done the same since MM-155 and MM-257, so the sibling was followed; whether both move onto a surface (`MmListRow entry` in a group, MM-291) or the exception is written into the direction is the owner's, after a render. The guard found nothing blocking in the entry itself. Not changed here.
  - *The fortnight of chips stands before the fields; the chips carry hours* (PD-3 medium) and *the same chip means a fact in Period days and a cursor in Sleep* (PD-5 medium): **kept as drawn, reason written** in MM-250's Decisions: the guard's walkthrough found that a chip's hours are how a novice tells a recorded night from an unrecorded one (step 4 passes) and that the pre-picked "Last night" makes the daily task one scroll; the order and the chips' hours are listed in MM-416 for the owner's look after a render.
  - *"Not recorded" over "No nights recorded yet" says one thing twice* (PD-9 low): **accepted**; the detail now names where nights come from.
  - *The caption is the schema's phrasing* (PD-11 low): **accepted**; "Each night goes under the morning it ended, so last night is the one that ended today." The morning convention stays (MM-250 Notes).
  - *The folded recovery line is two sentences* (PD-10 low), *Progress exceeds its group budget* (PD-12 low, pre-existing), *chip text may be spoken as letters* (F8 hypothesis): **deferred to MM-416's list**; a render and a device pass decide them.
  - *The ticket's "same stepper the other entries use"* (both): **accepted**; MM-250's Decisions say no entry uses a stepper and why fields were built.
