## Novice mobile experience (when UX/product design is assigned)
Depth target 1 (walked, from source only; no render): a person on their first morning with the Sleep section, no watch, typing last night and then correcting the night before.

Sleep sits after Weight events (and Period days for a female profile), below the first viewport; its header is the bedtime icon, "Sleep", caption "Last night not recorded yet.", chevron open. The routine control is the last thing in the section. Passes (the control is findable) with a scroll cost (PD-3).
- Step 2, which night. "Last night" is pre-picked (check on the first chip). Nothing near the fields says which night they belong to; the group's name is aria-only (SleepNightCard.tsx:104, MmDurationField.tsx:23). Fails for a novice who scrolled to the fields (PD-4).
- Step 3, type 7 and 20. Plain dense number fields with the unit as suffix; the Save border strengthens when valid (ui.css:35–37, 66–68). Passes.
- Step 4, Save. Focus blurs, the record is written, the first chip becomes "Last night · 7 h 20 m", the note becomes "1 of the last 14 nights recorded.", the header caption becomes "Last night 7 h 20 m." (sleepEntry.test.tsx:62–75). No snack. Passes; feedback is split across three places none of which is beside Save (PD-13, low).

## Findings

**PD-1 — Two fields and a Save sit bare on the canvas inside the open Sleep section.** Domain: product-designer, with react-capacitor. Class: confirmed design-rule violation (screen-direction rule 1 as amended by MM-291: "Anything that holds a control sits on a surface… a bare canvas section carries read-only rows only"; `hierarchyProblems` encodes it). Severity: high. Confidence: high that the rule is broken in the SleepNotRecorded state; medium on whether the owner regards the Progress Waist section, which does the same when opened (WaistEntryCard.tsx:60–77), as a standing exception; no written one found in `docs/agent-runs/mm291-weigh-in/decision.md` or MM-250. Where: SleepNightCard.tsx:84, 102–112; MmSection.tsx:33–38, 144–148; hierarchyProblems.ts:26–29; hierarchy.test.tsx:27 (only destination stories run); DemoSeed.ts:68, 108 (today recorded, so the demo folds Sleep and the check never sees the open state). Expected: the entry (fields, Save, note) on a surface, as the Dashboard weigh-in and waist rows are (`MmListRow entry` in a group, MM-291/MM-301), or in a sheet. Observed: `.mm-field` directly in `.mm-section-body` on the canvas, in the most frequent state. The sibling matches (Waist), so rule 9 is satisfied while rule 1 is not; the rule-9 instruction is then to ask whether the sibling is right, not to copy it. Remediation (proposal): draw the entry as an `MmListGroup` with one `MmListRow entry` holding `MmDurationField`, Save and the note, inside the section (the pattern `WaistRow` uses, `apps/mobile/src/dashboard/WaistRow.tsx`); apply the same to Waist in a follow-up ticket, or record the exception in the ticket and in `design/screen-direction.md`. Acceptance: `hierarchyProblems` over the SleepNotRecorded story (and SleepFromWatch, SleepMistyped) returns no bare-field problem; extend `hierarchy.test.tsx` to run those stories or every `ProgressScreen` story. Verification: inspection only.

**PD-2 — The Save's placement differs from Waist without a written reason, and likely misaligns with the fields.** Class: design/usability improvement plus documentation gap (rule 9). Severity: medium. Confidence: high on the divergence; medium on the misalignment (CSS reading, no render). Where: progress.css:66–74 (`.progress-sleep-entry { align-items: flex-start }`) versus 32–41 (`.progress-waist-save`); MM-250.md Decisions (names Waist, gives no reason for the arrangement). Observed: Save beside the field column, which begins with the "Hours"/"Minutes" labels so the 48dp field boxes start about 20px lower than the 48dp Save; an error under Hours lengthens the column further. Remediation (proposal): Waist's layout (fields in a row; note and Save on the row beneath, note flex 1), which also gives the note the width it needs. Acceptance: the SleepNotRecorded and SleepMistyped stories in the three palettes show the Save on one baseline with its neighbours; MM-250 Decisions names the arrangement and why. Verification: render needed.

**PD-3 — The fortnight picker stands between the heading and the routine control.** Class: design/usability improvement (anti-cruft §7 "routine action and minimum context first"; screen-direction rule 7). Severity: medium. Confidence: medium (widths estimated from tokens; a recorded date chip "Sat, Oct 17 · 6 h 40 m" is roughly 180px, so two per row at best; Period days' "Sat, Oct 17" fits three per row). Where: SleepNightCard.tsx:89–101 before 102–112; progress.css:60–65. Observed: in the SleepRecorded state the chips alone are seven to fourteen rows of 48dp, and the chip row grows as more nights are recorded. The chips are a correction tool read on the rare path, placed on the frequent path. Remediation (alternatives for the owner): (a) fields and Save first, with "Last night" named beside them (PD-4), and the fortnight after them or behind an `MmDisclosure` "Another night"; (b) keep the order but take the hours off the chips, since the picked night's hours already fill the fields and the note already says them for an imported night, which also makes the chips as short and regular as Period days'; (c) both. Acceptance: in SleepRecorded, the Hours field is within one viewport of the Sleep header at 412 by 915 in the story. Verification: render needed.

**PD-4 — Nothing beside the fields names the night they belong to.** Class: usability problem found by inspection (steps 2 and 5). Severity: medium (met every time a night other than last night is corrected; recurs). Confidence: high. Where: SleepNightCard.tsx:102–112; MmDurationField.tsx:23 (`aria-label={name}` only); FieldsCatalog.tsx:52. Expected: a visible label for the duration group ("Last night", "The night before", "Sat, Oct 17"), as the check-in gives its instance a visible `SectionLabel`. Observed: the only visible tie between the picked chip and the fields is the check on a chip that may be several rows above. User impact: a value typed for the wrong night, unnoticed, since Save's name is "Save night" with no night in it. Remediation (proposal): give `MmDurationField` an optional visible `label` and pass `label(picked)`; or write the picked night at the head of the note in every case, not only when imported: "The night before: not recorded yet." Acceptance: in SleepRecorded after pressing "The night before", the words "The night before" appear within the entry row or its note; the UX guard confirms the heard name does not then double.

**PD-5 — The same chip means a fact in Period days and a cursor in Sleep, one section apart, with no written reason.** Class: usability problem found by inspection, and a rule-9 documentation gap. Severity: medium (met by every female profile; the two groups are adjacent). Confidence: high on the divergence; the confusion is analytical, not observed. Where: PeriodDaysCard.tsx:43 versus SleepNightCard.tsx:91–99; MmChoiceChip.tsx:11–26 (one appearance). Observed: an unrecorded picked night ("The night before" with the check) looks like a marked period day. Remediation (alternatives): write the reason in MM-250 Decisions and rely on the hours text as the record's mark; or, with PD-3(b), make the chips a pure picker so the check is the only state and reads as "chosen"; or, with PD-3(a), demote the picker so the two groups are not adjacent equals. No new chip variant proposed (anti-cruft §5). Acceptance: MM-250 Decisions records the choice; the UX guard's heard-name review covers `aria-pressed` meaning "picked".

**PD-6 — A minutes error marks the Hours field.** Class: implementation defect found by inspection (not runtime-reproduced). Severity: medium (every mistyped minute; points at the wrong box). Confidence: high. Where: MmDurationField.tsx:31 (`error={error}` on Hours only) and 35–44 (no `error` on Minutes); SleepNightCard.tsx:51; sleepEntry.test.tsx:107–108 asserts only the text, not which field is `aria-invalid`. Expected: the field whose value is wrong carries the danger border and `aria-invalid` (MM-279). Observed: "Minutes from 0 to 59." is drawn under Hours with the Hours box in danger. Remediation (proposal): split the prop into `hoursError` and `minutesError`, and have SleepNightCard route each message; the Fields catalog gets the minutes-error state. Acceptance: a test that types 75 minutes and asserts the Minutes textbox is `aria-invalid` and the Hours one is not; a story state for it. A bug under AGENTS.md: ticket and regression test together.

**PD-7 — At the check-in the same control refuses nothing; a mistyped value is dropped silently.** Class: usability problem found by inspection; missing state and story (MM-292). Severity: medium-low (the field is optional and pre-filled). Confidence: high on the code path. Where: RecoveryCheckInScreen.tsx:47–58 (hours > 24 or ≤ 0 becomes `undefined`), 93–98 (no `error`), 99–101 (the caption still says "Your average…" after the person has typed something else). Expected: the same control behaves the same on both screens; the person sees why a value was not kept. Remediation (proposal): reuse SleepNightCard's two checks, show the error in the field, keep Save off while it stands; when `typedSleep` is set, change the caption to say the typed value is what will be kept. Acceptance: a `RecoveryCheckInMistyped` story; a test that 25 h at the check-in is refused in place.

**PD-8 — While the nights load or if the read fails, the Sleep section vanishes.** Class: documentation or requirement gap, with a missing story. Severity: medium (a failed read shown as nothing; screen-direction "Error": a failed read is never shown as zero or as an empty success). Confidence: medium. Where: SleepNightCard.tsx:38–41; the same pattern in PeriodDaysCard.tsx:25 (pre-existing). Stories: none for `failingRead('sleep', …)` or `waitingRead`. Expected: layout reserved while loading; a `ReadFailedNotice` for the section when the read fails, as the lift list has. **PD-9 — "Not recorded" over "No nights recorded yet" says one thing twice, and the Coach cites a fact without a way to its home.** Class: design/usability improvement (anti-cruft "no narration: restates"; screen-direction rule 4). Severity: low. Where: RecoveryCard.tsx:46, 52–56. Remediation (proposal): with no nights, detail "Typed on Progress, or read from your watch" (the route) or no detail. Not a blocker.

**PD-10 — The folded recovery line is two sentences and will likely wrap.** Class: design/usability improvement (rule 1: "an optional one-line summary"). Severity: low. Confidence: medium (estimate, no render). Where: RecoveryCard.tsx:41–45. Remediation (proposal): when sleep exists and the check-in is not due, consider the sleep figure alone, since "Last answered" is history available inside. Owner's call. Acceptance: the CoachSleepLine story folded shows one line at 412 wide in the three palettes.

**PD-11 — A date chip names a morning; the caption carries the whole explanation.** Class: usability problem found by inspection, low severity (met only when correcting a night older than two days; MM-250 Notes fixes the convention, so a change is a requirement change).
