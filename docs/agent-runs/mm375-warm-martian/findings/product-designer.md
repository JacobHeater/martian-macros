# Review: product-designer, run `mm375-warm-martian`

Saved by the chair, as returned by the read-only reviewer (Claude Opus 5.5 subagent, 2026-10-17). Planning mode. The
chair verified every figure by computer (`verify.mjs`, scratchpad); they agree with the hand arithmetic to 0.1.

## Measurement of the washout (old Martian, CIE LCh)
All eleven neutrals and both secondary text colours sit between hue 301° and 309° (a spread of 8.2°) at chroma 21-35;
fat (305°) sits in the same band: 12 of 21 values, covering nearly the whole screen. Fat and its track differ only by
lightness. Warm accents: pink 351°, coral 27°, gold 86°; nothing between 30° and 80°; in the Food and Dashboard renders the
only non-violet colours are lime and aqua.

## Research (WebSearch, WebFetch; accessed 2026-10-17)
- AOL, "Nickelodeon's splat is back…", updated 2023-06-09: the 1984 splat was Pantone 021 orange; lime and orange were
  chosen as colours adults disliked; used until 2009. Supports lime with tangerine.
- 99% Invisible, "Jazz Cups…", Kurt Kohlstedt, 2020-11-19: teal and purple encapsulate the early-90s look; Martian is
  already this pairing, and the purple is what is overused.
- Envato, "The rise and fall (and return) of Memphis patterns", 2024-12-03: clashing brights turning neon in the 90s; the
  desaturated "Memphis Lite" register is what dated, and the lavender captions are close to it.
- Not retrieved: Domus, Triennale (403), Creative Bloq (paywall). No claim about sportswear.

## Proposal (as returned)
Neutrals to plum/aubergine (hue about 329°) at about 45% less chroma, L* steps kept; text2/text3 at chroma 10-14; lime
kept; carbs tangerine `#FF9A3D`, fat hot magenta `#E84FAE`; protein and ion kept aqua; states kept; grade fills
categorical: A `#FFE45E`, B `#3FD08A`, C `#A6B4FF`, D `#FF9EDB`, ink on all. Doc rules overruled: §1.3 restated as one
filled action; grade fills share hue families with caution, ion, info and fat (no room on the wheel), mitigated by
lettered pills never on charts.

## Findings
- PD-1 (medium, high confidence): the washout is one hue at chroma 21-35 across all neutrals and secondary text.
- PD-2 (medium): MM-370's "neutral outlined badge" conflicts with the owner's fills; amend.
- PD-3 (hypothesis): four bright fills on every food row may compete with the lime action; judge rendered.

## Requests
Coaching/safety: does any coloured grade fill break MM-108's "state, do not judge"; does a categorical rainbow avoid it
better than a ramp. UX: run the Machado simulation; judge text2 as "dusty pink". Product: is lower chroma still Martian
(alternative B keeps the grape hue).

## Chair's outcome
The plum was drawn and rejected by the owner ("turned maroon … not a 90's purple"). Built instead: a royal 90s violet
(bluer than the grape), larger steps, near-neutral secondary text (not the proposed pink-lilac), the proposed tangerine,
magenta and grade fills. Machado simulation run by the chair (see the colour doc §1). The coaching/safety question on grade
fills stays open for when MM-370 is built.
