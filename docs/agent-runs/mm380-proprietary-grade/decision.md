# Panel decision: a food grade of the app's own (MM-380), fuller food packs (MM-381)

- **Facts versus proposals and traceability.** Four read-only reports (nutrition, micronutrients, measurement, integrations) at 42ce258 plus the working tree; the micronutrients role read the tree after the chair had begun the build, and says so. The chair ran the draft over a calibration set (`test/support/gradeCalibrationFoods.ts`), measured the published packs with node:sqlite, and wrote `docs/food-grades.md` and `docs/food-packs-data.md`.
- **Coverage.** The draft method, the engine as built, the data shapes, the pipeline and its sources, the register, the owner's tickets. Not read by any role: the FNDDS documentation PDFs, the FDC data dictionary (blocked), the FDA label rule by the integrations role (read by measurement and micronutrients). No role ran a test; the chair ran them.
- **Verdicts.** All four: build with the listed changes.
- **Findings and disposition.**
  - *Energy density counts twice and decides the bottom; dry and cooked states a letter apart* (NU-1, ME-5): **accepted**, reconciled: the list starts at 3 kcal/g (nutrition) and stops at 5 points (measurement's cap of four, nutrition's five; five taken, under fiber's seven): 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.5.
  - *The sugars waiver is a cliff on the least reliable input and splits dairy and fruit* (ME-4, NU-2): **accepted**, reconciled: nutrition's three gates (watery, fiber 1 g, protein 4.5 g) as measurement's ramps (fiber 0.5 to 1, protein 2.25 to 4.5, the waiver fading between 1.5 and 2 kcal/g), the kept points rounded half up.
  - *Per-calorie sodium amplifies rounding below 50 kcal; a brine nets out* (ME-3, ME-6, NU-3): **accepted in part**: the denominator floor applies to what counts against a food, at 40 kcal (measurement's 50 demoted every raw vegetable, which nutrition's order puts at the top; 40 keeps cola at D); what counts for a food stays per 100 kcal; the sodium scale runs to 14,000 mg so soy sauce reads C. Declined: a per-100 g sodium backstop (NU-3's decision 3), recorded for after the first pack build.
  - *The D bands were drawn to the old energy scale* (NU-6): **accepted**: D+ ≥ −5, D ≥ −8.
  - *Threshold convention; rounding and direction* (ME-7, ME-9): **accepted**: "reaches" for the lists that count for, "passes" for those against; the roll-up rounds half up; the letter is the highest step whose floor the score reaches; tested at every threshold and cut.
  - *The micronutrient mean's denominator; a sparse label inflates; four stated is the law* (MN-1, MN-2, MN-3, ME-8): **accepted**: over the stated, bounded by breadth at 5% of the Daily Value per 100 kcal, counted from four.
  - *Fortified sweetened cereal reaches B* (NU-9, MN-4): **accepted** as nutrition's gate: at most three points when counted sugars pass 8 g per 100 kcal; fortification otherwise counts as the label states it (MN-4), disclosed.
  - *The popover's "not stated" is false for a label that states four* (MN-7): **accepted**: the grade carries how many of the ten are stated and the popover says "Only 3 of the 10 …" or "counted from the 4 stated".
  - *The calibration set lacks inputs and a food on every cut* (ME-1, NU-5): **accepted**: 61 foods with every input, at least two on every step, the sensitivity bounds (5% one step, 20% two) and the order checks nutrition asked for, as tests.
  - *Nuts and oils; refined grains; lean red meat* (NU-7, NU-10): **accepted as disclosed limitations**; an unsaturated-fat positive declined for a US launch without a trans-fat field.
  - *Recipes cannot be graded as MM-382 said* (NU-11): **accepted**: a recipe keeps the question mark naming fiber and sodium; MM-382's decision corrected.
  - *Pack foods graded without sugars, saturated fat or micronutrients: cola C on day one* (NU-4): **the owner's decision stands** (macros, fiber and sodium required; refinements when stated), recorded in `docs/food-grades.md` with the coverage; the fix is the format 2 rebuild (MM-381, MM-383).
  - *The Daily Values' potassium and calcium gaps; a label zero* (MN-5, MN-8): **accepted**, recorded in the register rows.
  - *Stale USDA pins; FNDDS as a third source; trust tier for a new source; generic duplicates; coverage per source; CC0 confirmed; Experimental and the Canadian file declined* (IN-1 to IN-10, MN-10): **accepted**, built under MM-383 (owner's instruction of 2026-10-18 to add a source): FNDDS 2021-2023 joins the generic branch as `reference`, an unknown source fails the build, generic foods with one normalised name keep the most reliable source's, `coverage.txt` counts per pack and per source with the grade-readiness rows, the pack terms state CC0 with USDA's citation. A category column stays a format 3 follow-up.
- **Chair's measurements.** On the packs published 2026-10-09: generic 7,270 of 8,088 gradeable on the new method (fiber and sodium stated, 10 kcal or more), 99 under 10 kcal, 1,138 under 50 kcal, fiber stated as 0 for 3,370; barcode 377,606 of 456,191 gradeable, 23,065 under 10 kcal, 67,399 under 50 kcal, fiber 0 for 138,807.
- **Owner decisions taken by the chair ("say if any is wrong", recorded in MM-380):** the 40 kcal floor on the against side only; the waiver's ramps and gates; energy density to five points; no sodium backstop per 100 g; protein's weight stays eight; no unsaturated-fat positive; the fortification gate; four stated; the breadth bound; recipes stay ungraded; the eating-disorder decision of MM-370 stands on the new method (nutrition's safety note: every A+ is a vegetable) and is flagged to the owner.
