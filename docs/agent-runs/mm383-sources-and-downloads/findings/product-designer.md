## Novice mobile experience
Bounded goal: a person new to the app opens Settings > Food database to make barcode scanning work; two packs are offered; she downloads both, goes to the Dashboard, comes back, one has finished, and she pauses the other. Decisions counted from source: 1 press (See what is available), 2 choices (which pack(s)), 2 presses, 1 Back, 1 Cancel, then Resume or Discard: 7 inputs, no branches except the host failing.

Walkthrough (labels: **walked** = step record from source and a render; **surveyed** = source only):
1. **Surveyed.** Food database, nothing downloaded: intro sentence, "On this phone: Nothing downloaded yet", "Download" header, secondary "See what is available" with its one-line disclosure (`FoodDatabaseScreen.tsx:84-99`). Sign of result: the spinner line. Passes.
2. **Walked** (`food-database-available-*`). Two cards: "Common foods", "Barcode foods, United States", each 267 px tall; the second card ends at y 862 and the next header is at the viewport's foot. What she must decide: which pack she needs; the intro says scanning "needs a database of foods" and the names carry the rest. What she must find: the one action. The only control on each card is a 24 px arrow-to-tray glyph in text color, sitting beside the muted sentence "It is stored on this phone and used without a connection", 65 px below the title, 12 px short of the content edge. The glyph's shape is conventional and legible in all three palettes, so the step passes, but it passes on shape alone: nothing in color, position or weight says "this is the action", and the Remove sibling a hundred pixels above is louder (PD-1, PD-2). Both cards repeat the same five-line paragraph (PD-3).
3. **Walked** (`food-database-two-downloading-*`). Pressing both: each card is replaced by a "Downloading" card (name, bar, "2 kB of 4 kB", outlined Cancel) and a strip line appears at the foot for each. Two signs of result per press. Passes. What she sees: two cards both headed "Downloading", the names in body text (PD-4), and four Cancel buttons on one screen (two outlined in the cards, two Ember text in the strip), each beside its name, so each is findable; the density is notable (see Q2).

## Findings

**PD-1** (product-designer) — design/usability improvement — severity medium, confidence high (render + sibling source). Rule: screen-direction rule 9 (like its siblings), MM-384's own Decisions ("so the card reads as a row").
Observed: the download icon is centered beside two text lines mid-card (`AvailablePackCard.tsx:24-32`, `foodPacks.css:16-24`), nearest the muted storage sentence, not the title and not the card's end; the privacy paragraph beneath runs full width so the icon floats in the card's middle (render `food-database-available-martian`, icon at x 355, y 405; title at y 340; content edge x 379). The ticket says "beside the size and version"; the render says beside both lines, visually paired with the storage sentence.
Expected: the app's established place for "the one action on this thing" is the title row or the row's end: `WorkoutExerciseCard.tsx:125-127` (the only other `InfoCard` with an icon action, in `trailing`), `WeightEventsCard.tsx:85-99` and `WaterSection.tsx:118` (section headers), `MyFoodsStep.tsx:68` (a row's `trailingAction`).
Change: pass the icon as `InfoCard`'s `trailing` so its center is level with the title; delete `.food-packs-offer` and `.food-packs-offer-text`; the two text lines then run full width (the first no longer wraps "without a connection." early). Update MM-384's Decisions sentence to "in the card's title row, like the exercise card's Remove and a section header's add".
Acceptance: on `FoodDatabaseAvailable` the icon's vertical center is within 4 px of the title's; no icon sits beside body text.

**PD-2** (product-designer) — design/usability improvement — severity medium, confidence high (render, `ui.css:39-43,70-86`). Rule: hierarchy (screen-direction rule 6: text actions and header actions are the non-primary tier, and the design system marks them with Ember); MM-276 (Ember never a tint).
Observed: the constructive action the screen exists for is a glyph in `--text`, the same color as the body copy; the destructive sibling 100 px above (Remove, `kind="text"`) is Ember. The screen's hierarchy is inverted: remove is louder than get.
Change: give `MmIconButton` a kind whose glyph is Ember and whose plate is none (say `kind="action"`: `.mm-icon-button-action { color: var(--ember) }`, pressed state as today's `.mm-pressed .mm-icon-button`), and use it here. This is the icon form of `.mm-button-text`, a solid glyph, not a wash, so MM-276 holds. Do not reuse `kind="add"`: it is hard-coded to the plus (`MmIconButton.tsx:31-33`) and its plate is the owner's one approved Add exception (screen-direction, "Both themes").
Acceptance: the download glyph and the Remove label are the same color in each palette; `StatesCatalog.tsx` gains the kind's default, pressed and disabled states. Contrast of Ember on `--surface` in Light to the UX guard (Q2); Ember text on a surface is already how Remove is drawn, so I expect no new exposure.

**PD-3** (product-designer) — design/usability improvement — severity medium, confidence medium (render; depends on Q1). Rule: screen-direction rule 7 (the first viewport answers the question), anti-cruft (repeated text).
Observed: `AvailablePackCard.tsx:33-36` repeats the identical five-line host/IP/mobile-data paragraph in every offered card (two now; three with MM-383); each card is 267 px, of which the paragraph is about 110. The second card's foot is at y 862 of 915.
Change: when every offered listing has the same `url.host` (true for the one manifest host), say the paragraph once, muted, under the "Download" header above the first card, naming the host; fall back to per-card only when hosts differ. The card keeps the size/version line, the storage line and the icon. The disclosure stays visible before any press, above the first card, so nothing is hidden. Alternative if Q1 says the words must sit with each button: keep per-card but cut the card's storage sentence into the same paragraph so each card is one paragraph, not two.
Acceptance: with two packs offered, both cards fit in the first viewport with "Where the foods come from" visible beneath.

**PD-4** (product-designer) — design/usability improvement — severity medium, confidence high (render `food-database-two-downloading-*`). Rule: screen-direction rule 4 (one home per fact), rule 9 (siblings on the same screen).
Observed: the running card's heading is the phase ("Downloading", `PackDownloadCard.tsx:10,27`); with two downloads both cards are headed identically and the only differing line is body text. On the same screen the pack's name is the heading of the offer card and the title of the installed row. The paused card does the same ("Paused", line 39).
Change: heading = the pack's title; first line = `Downloading · 2 kB of 4 kB` (phase word then bytes) above the bar, or the phase word as the line and bytes under the bar as now; Paused likewise (`Paused · 2 kB of 4 kB kept on your phone…`). Then the card and its strip line ("Downloading Common foods") say the same thing in the same order.
Acceptance: on `FoodDatabaseTwoDownloading` the two card headings differ; `phaseTitle` is no longer a heading.

**PD-5** (product-designer, refer react-capacitor) — unverified hypothesis requiring investigation (mechanism read from CSS; not reproduced on a device) — severity medium, confidence high in the mechanism, low in its size on the owner's phone.
Observed: `ui.css:1280` gives every `.mm-download-strip` `padding-bottom: calc(8px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`. With two lines stacked (`DownloadStatusHost.tsx:20-36`), the upper line carries the inset as a blank band between the two lines. The tab bar also carries the inset (`ui.css:148`) while the strip sits beneath it (`App.tsx:81-87`), which was already so with one line (pre-existing, outside MM-384's diff but on its path). In headless Chrome the inset is 0, so the captures cannot show it; the shell reports a real height "safe area included" (`MmNavigationBar.tsx:15`).
Change: one container around the lines (`.mm-download-strips`, `background: var(--raised)`, `padding-bottom: env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`), each line without the inset; and whichever surface is lowest on screen is the only one that carries the inset (if the strip shows, the tab bar's inset padding should collapse: a `--mm-download-strip-shown` property or the host setting a class on the root, the react-capacitor role's call).
Verification requested: the phone checklist already carries "both packs downloaded together on a release build"; add "with gesture navigation, no blank band between the two strip lines nor between the tab bar and the strip".

**PD-6** (product-designer) — design/usability improvement — severity medium, confidence high (measured in `food-database-two-downloading-*` and `download-under-the-dashboard-*`; CSS `ui.css:1276-1292`, `.mm-button` min-height 48 at line 19).
Observed: each strip line is 64 px (the Cancel's 48 target plus 8 + 8). Two lines = 128 px; under the 72 px tab bar that is 200 px (22%) of the 915 px first viewport on every tab screen for as long as both run; with a third pack (MM-383) 264 px (29%). On the pushed Food database screen two lines take 14%. The text column needs 28 px (label 16 + gap 4 + thin bar).
Does a stacked strip steal too much? At 64 px a line, two lines are tolerable for a state the person started and can end; three are not. At 48 px a line, two or three are fine.
Change: the line's vertical padding to 0 with `min-height: 48px` (the Cancel target sets the height; the text column centers), a 1 px `var(--outline)` hairline between lines (`.mm-download-strip + .mm-download-strip { border-top: … }`), and the inset once on the container (PD-5). Two lines then 97 px, three 146.
Alternative (owner decision D4, not recommended): one summary line when more than one runs ("Downloading 2 food databases · 48%", Cancel opening the Food database screen). It loses the per-download Cancel that MM-384's acceptance criteria and the UX guard's brief ask for.
Acceptance: a strip line measures 48 px without the inset; two lines under the Dashboard leave the water row's top visible at 412x915.

**PD-7** (product-designer) — design/usability improvement — severity low, confidence high (render `download-under-the-dashboard-light`). Rule: screen-direction "Bottom navigation": `raised` surface with a 1 dp `outline` top hairline.
Observed: the strip is `raised` with no top hairline (`ui.css:1276-1282`); under the tab bar in Light both are white and fuse into one 136 px block with nothing but the tab labels to tell the bar from the strip. Pre-existing with one line, worse with two.
Change: `border-top: 1px solid var(--outline)` on the strip container.

**PD-8** (product-designer) — design/usability improvement — severity low, confidence medium (render `food-database-installed-*`, source `FoodDatabaseScreen.tsx:110-128`, `PackDownloadCard.tsx:61-65`). Rule: screen-direction rule 4 (one home per fact).

The Notice is the press's result signal and MM-385 ties it to Remove, so it is defensible; it is still a repeat on one screen. Option for the owner (D5): drop the Notice when the screen is left (the row remains the home), or keep as built. Not a change I insist on.

**PD-9** (product-designer) — documentation or requirement gap — severity low, confidence high. MM-384 UI/UX names "DownloadUnderTheDashboard (two lines)", but the story (`Settings.stories.tsx:542-552`) walks `download` (476-479), which starts only Barcode foods; all three `download-under-the-dashboard-*` renders show one line. Change: a `TwoDownloadsUnderTheDashboard` story (both icons, Back) or change the existing walk, and recapture; also recapture `FoodDatabasePaused` and `FoodDatabaseOneDone` after their walks settle (the supplied captures are mid-walk and identical).

**PD-10** (product-designer; tone to coaching) — design/usability improvement (visual copy) — severity low, confidence medium. `FoodGrade.tsx:20`: "from what it gives per calorie … calories per gram count against it" asks a novice to hold two denominators in one sentence; "calories per gram" is the one phrase she must translate (it means a calorie-dense food). The amount step's caption "Grade for the food itself, per calorie" (`amount-step-with-grade-*`) reads well beside the badge and matches the source line's weight above it; no change.

**PD-12** (product-designer) — design/usability improvement, against a written rule — severity medium, confidence high (source `PackDownloadCard.tsx:46,57`; not rendered: no story with two paused or two failed). Rule: screen-direction rule 6 (at most one Ember fill in view).
