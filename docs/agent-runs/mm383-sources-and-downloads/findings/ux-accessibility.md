## Findings

**F1. Each strip line's `role="status"` announces every percent change for the whole download, on every screen, and two downloads double it.** Domain: ux-accessibility (fix in react-capacitor's component). Class: usability problem found by inspection. Severity: **high, blocking** (met by every screen-reader user on every download; lasts minutes; recurs; cannot be dismissed except by cancelling the download). Confidence: medium-high. The mechanism is in the source: `DownloadStatusStrip.tsx:25-27` renders `{label}, {round(fraction*100)} percent` inside a status region; React rewrites the text node whenever the rounded integer changes, so a 46.7 MB pack produces up to 100 changes, two packs up to 200, interleaved. The claim that a status role is a polite, atomic live region that speaks each change is from my knowledge of ARIA, not a retrieved source (research was disallowed; see Research); TalkBack's queuing of rapid polite updates is unobserved. Either way the person is told "Downloading Barcode foods, United States, 44 percent … 45 percent …" while trying to log food on the Dashboard, and with a fast connection the updates arrive faster than they can be spoken. The React role asked exactly this question; my answer is yes, announce less often. MM-384's criterion "each strip line is its own status with the pack's name and percent" is where the requirement would change.
Expected: the person hears that a download started, hears coarse progress, and hears when it changes phase or ends. Observed (source): every integer percent.
Fix (concrete, small): in `DownloadStatusStrip.tsx` keep `role="status"` but render the hidden text at a coarse step, e.g. `const step = Math.floor(fraction * 10) * 10;` and `{label}, {step} percent`; the phase words ("Checking", "Installing") already change the label. Keep the exact figure where it is readable on demand: the card's "2 kB of 4 kB" (`PackDownloadCard.tsx:30-32`) is not live and stays. Update MM-384's criterion to "name and progress at ten-percent steps" and the test expectations at `foodDatabase.test.tsx:156, 271-274` (22 → 20 percent, 43 → 40 percent; 50 stays). Acceptance: a test drives a download through 22 then 28 percent and asserts the status text changed once. Whether the step is 10 or 25 percent is the owner's call (decision 1).

**F2. Pressing the icon, the card's Cancel, or confirming Remove unmounts the focused control and leaves focus on the document body.** Domain: ux-accessibility. Class: usability problem found by inspection. Severity: medium (keyboard, switch and screen-reader users; once per action; recoverable by one swipe or Tab, and the live region or the visible card gives the sign). Confidence: high (source). `AvailablePackCard.tsx:31` + `FoodDatabaseScreen.tsx:63-69` (icon → card removed); `PackDownloadCard.tsx:33` (Cancel → card re-rendered as Paused without the button); `FoodDatabaseScreen.tsx:34-47` with `MmDialog.tsx:30` returning focus to a Remove button that no longer exists. The repository already names this rule and has the helper (`ui/focusNeighbourOf.ts:29-35`, WCAG 2.4.3, MM-379).
Fix: hold a ref to the pressed control and call `focusNeighbourOf(ref.current)` before `start`, `cancel` and after the confirmed `remove` (the three sites above). Preferred for the icon: a neighbour lands on the other pack's icon or the Back control; better still would be the new card's Cancel, but that needs a mount-time focus in `PackDownloadCard` guarded so it does not steal focus when the screen is merely opened with a download running; I would take `focusNeighbourOf` now. Acceptance: tests assert `document.activeElement` is a button inside the screen (not `document.body`) after each of the three actions. Not blocking.

**F3. The grade popover has no height cap or scrolling; with the not-counted sentences at large text it overflows the screen and "Got it" is unreachable.** Domain: ux-accessibility (design-system component). Class: usability problem found by inspection (reasoned; no render at 200%). Severity: medium (large-text users; every grade press on a food missing sugars, saturated fat or micronutrients, which per MM-382's context is most pack foods today; recovery exists via Escape or the scrim but is undiscoverable by touch). Confidence: medium. `ui.css:996-1007` (fixed, no `max-height`/`overflow`), `MmPopover.tsx:20-22, 42-46` (width capped to the viewport, height assumed 160 px). Arithmetic: five sentences ≈ 500 characters; at 200% the body is about 30 px with 44 px lines in a 288 px bubble on a 320 px screen, roughly 25 lines ≈ 1,100 px before title and button. At 100% the same text is about 380 px and fits.
Fix: `.mm-popover { max-height: calc(100vh - 32px); overflow-y: auto; }` and clamp `top`/`bottom` so the bubble never starts off-screen; keep "Got it" reachable by making the actions row sticky or by letting the panel scroll to it. Acceptance: a story of the five-sentence popover (a food with no sugars, saturated fat or micronutrients stated) rendered at a 320×568 viewport with 200% text shows "Got it". Not blocking.

**F4. A finished download is not announced.** Domain: ux-accessibility. Class: design/usability improvement. Severity: low. Confidence: high (source). The status region for the pack is removed and the "is on your phone" `Notice` has no live role (`PackDownloadCard.tsx:61-65`, `Notice.tsx:30-44`). A screen-reader user learns of completion only by finding the notice or noticing the silence. Fix: wrap the finished notice (or render a hidden sibling) with `role="status"` once, so "Common foods is on your phone." is spoken on arrival; same for the failed notice. Owner decision 2 covers whether completion should also be told on other screens (a snack), which MM-56 did not ask for.

**F5. Cancelling from the strip on another screen gives no route to Resume.** Domain: ux-accessibility, with product. Class: design/usability improvement (pre-existing, MM-56). Severity: low. The line vanishes (`DownloadStatusHost.tsx:14`); the Paused card is in Settings › Food database. A novice who tapped Cancel by mistake has no sign of where the kept bytes went. Possible fix: a snack "Paused. Resume in Settings › Food database." Owner decision 3.

**F6. Two stacked strip lines at 200% text on a 320 px screen take a large share of the viewport.** Domain: product-designer, with ux-accessibility. Class: unverified hypothesis requiring investigation (no render at that size). Severity: low-medium while two downloads run. Reasoning: each line is a label in `--type-label` (0.8125rem, `tokens.css:169`) that wraps inside a flex column (`ui.css:1283-1292`, no `nowrap`) beside a Cancel text button (min 48 px, `ui.css:39-43`); at 200% "Downloading Barcode foods, United States" is two or three lines of about 26 px, plus the 4 px bar and 16 px padding: roughly 80-100 px per line, 160-200 px for two, above the 64 px navigation, on a viewport of perhaps 568 px. Usable, but worth a render. Request to the chair: capture DownloadUnderTheDashboard with two lines at 320×568 and 200% text. A mitigation if it is too much is the owner's: one summary line ("Downloading 2 packs", Cancel per pack only on the Food database screen) when more than one runs, which departs from MM-384's "a line per download" (decision 4).

**F7. The offer cards do not say what each pack is for.** Domain: product (requirement gap). Class: documentation or requirement gap. Severity: low. Both cards carry identical body copy (`AvailablePackCard.tsx:26-36`); only the screen intro mentions barcodes, and nothing says "Common foods" is the pack that search by name uses. MM-383's third source makes it three identical cards. The manifest has no description field, so this is a requirement for MM-383/MM-56, not a fix in MM-384. Route to the chair.

**F8. Wording notes.** Domain: ux-accessibility. Class: design/usability improvement. Severity: low. (a) "Try again Common foods" (`PackDownloadCard.tsx:57`) reads oddly but must start with the label; acceptable, or use "Try again: Common foods". (b) "Vitamins and minerals counted from the 4 stated." (`FoodGrade.tsx:50`) is a fragment; "Vitamins and minerals were counted from the 4 that are stated." (c) Two cards both headed "Downloading" (`PackDownloadCard.tsx:27`) are told apart only by body text; "Downloading Common foods" as the heading would also fix heading navigation. (d) The strip's visible text carries no figure, only the 4 px bar (`DownloadStatusStrip.tsx:28-31`); the hidden text has the percent. Pre-existing; fine for a glanceable strip.

Checked and passing (no finding): the icon's accessible names "Download Common foods" / "Update Barcode foods, United States" (`AvailablePackCard.tsx:21,31`; tests `:260-261, 242`); the 48 dp target (`ui.css:74-75`); every Cancel/Resume/Discard/Try again/Remove name starts with its label and names its pack (listed under facts); the double press (`PackDownloadsController.ts:60-67`); the kept-bytes sentence; the remove dialog's consequence text; the popover's focus in and out, Escape and scrim; "nothing is red" holds in all three palettes.

## Acceptance criteria and edge cases
Existing criteria traced: "The icon says what it does" → `foodDatabase.test.tsx:260-261` (names) and `ui.css:74-75` (target); "Cancel one, not the other" and "The strip for each" → `:269-279`; "Each strip line is told apart" → `:270-275` (passes as written; F1 asks it to be changed to coarse steps); "Removed" → `:283-300`; MM-382's popover sentences → `foodGradePopover.test.tsx:50-72`.
