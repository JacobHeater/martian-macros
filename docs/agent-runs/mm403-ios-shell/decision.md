# Panel decision: the app on an iPhone, where it differs (MM-403, MM-69)

- **Facts versus proposals and traceability.** The owner decided on 2026-10-19 (MM-403, MM-69): the DEMO identity, no "Close the app" on iOS, the platform named "Apple Health", a kind asked for counts as connected and "not allowed" is never claimed. Two read-only reviews of the working tree of `feat/ios-shell` (baseline `main` 08c11cf): the product designer and the UX guard, each given the brief and the stories as the render, read from source, since no browser capture could be made on the owner's Mac in this session. The chair (the parent session) edited, ran the tests and wrote this record. Both reviewers researched Apple's own pages for the premise (HealthKit hides read refusals: confirmed, Apple's `authorizationStatus(for:)` page) and for the route to an app's Health permissions.
- **Verdicts.** UX guard: **pass, no blocking finding**; the build proceeds (MM-293). Product designer: seven findings, one high.
- **Consolidated coverage.** Inspected: both components, their stories and tests, the Android siblings and the MM-320 run, the design-system parts, the four tickets, the direction documents. Not inspected by anyone: a browser render in any palette, a phone, VoiceOver, the Swift plugins, the `mm` tool. The owner's look at the stories in `mm storybook` remains the design check.
- **Findings and disposition** (deduplicated by cause; the designer's PD-n and the guard's Fn):
  - *The health section does not fold like its siblings* (PD-1 high, F4): **accepted, a regression of this branch.** `git show 08c11cf` of the old `HealthConnectSection.tsx` has `useSettingsSection('healthConnect')`; the rewrite lost it. Bug ticket **MM-407**, fixed: the fold is back on both returns, "Health Connect" joins the accordion test's list, and an iOS case checks "Apple Health" opens closed. Both tests fail on the tree before the fix.
  - *The recovery path "Health, Sharing, Apps" is not what Apple documents, and is written two ways* (PD-2 medium, F1 medium): **accepted.** The route is now the one Apple's security guide and privacy page document and that does not move between iOS versions, Settings, Health, Data Access & Devices, in one form in the row, the snack and the caption, and the row's sentence also covers the person who refused and wants to allow it now ("To allow it, or to check, open …"). Recorded in MM-69. The chair could not open the simulator's Health app (no taps through `simctl`); checking the route on the owner's phone, with its iOS version, is on the phone checklist. The guard's alternative, a row that opens the app's own page in Settings (`UIApplication.openSettingsURLString`), is possible and is left as an owner question in the handoff.
  - *The iOS caption outweighs its siblings and repeats the row* (PD-3): **accepted.** The general sentence is gone from the caption; the uncertainty is said in the row and the snack where it applies, and the caption keeps "The app only reads …" and "To stop, take the permission away in …", one sentence longer than Android's only by the route's name.
  - *"Everything allowed is up to date" on iOS* (PD-4, F3): **accepted.** On iOS the read-done snack says "Everything asked for is up to date."; a test and the story `AppleHealthReadDone` hold it.
  - *iOS states with words of their own have no story* (PD-5): **accepted in part.** `AppleHealthAsking` (a kind asked for and read) and `AppleHealthReadDone` are added. The asking-failed snack and "every kind asked" are not drawn: the former is the Android sibling's wording with the platform's name, the latter adds no words; declined with that reason.
  - *"device" and "phone" on one platform* (PD-6, F5): **accepted for the app's own words.** The shell is iPhone only (`TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1`, portrait), so the section says "phone" as the rest of the app does; the story is renamed `AppleHealthNotOnThisPhone`. The terms already say "phone"; Settings' erase dialog's "device" is pre-existing on both platforms and untouched.
  - *A state word on asked rows ("Asked. 5 readings …")* (PD-7, low confidence): **declined.** The guard walked the rows as they are and passed them; the count and the "Nothing found" sentence carry the state, and a word the person did not choose on Apple's sheet would be narration.
  - *", or leave the app from the Home Screen" reads as removing the app* (F2 medium): **accepted.** The sentence is now "… or go back to the Home Screen; the app will ask again when you open it." MM-403's criterion is reworded with it. Android's page keeps its button and sentence; the owner may want the second clause there too (handoff question).
  - *Focus after Apple's sheet and after "Not now"* (F6, hypothesis): **to the phone checklist**, with VoiceOver on; pre-existing on Android (MM-320 UX-3).
  - *Apple's sheet is a state no story can draw* (F7): **accepted**, a sentence in MM-69's UI/UX section.
  - The check mark's meaning on iOS: assessed by both, no finding; kept.
