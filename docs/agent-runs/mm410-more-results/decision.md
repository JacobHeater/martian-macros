# Panel decision: "More results" at the end of a food search (MM-410)

- **Facts versus proposals and traceability.** The owner asked on 2026-10-19 for the food search's scope to be widened; the 2026-10-19 packs (MM-409) made the top-twenty cap the remaining limit (MM-42's open item). Two read-only reviews of the working tree of `feat/more-results` (baseline `main` 2747fa9): the product designer and the UX guard, each given the brief and the two stories as the render, read from source. The chair (the parent session) edited, ran the tests and wrote this record. Both reviewers researched their premise (Baymard's load-more findings; WCAG 2.2 SC 4.1.3 and 2.4.3).
- **Verdicts.** UX guard: **pass, no blocking finding**; F1 high to fix before the build. Product designer: five findings, one medium.
- **Consolidated coverage.** Inspected: the component, its host, the two design-system parts, the hooks, the CSS, the stories, the tests, three tickets, both direction documents. Not inspected by anyone: a browser render in any palette, a phone, VoiceOver or TalkBack, Ember-on-raised contrast in Light, the ranking, the packs' data.
- **Findings and disposition** (deduplicated by cause):
  - *A screen reader gets no sign the list went on and loses focus when the button vanishes* (F1 high): **accepted.** The first new row takes focus (`MmListRow` gains `autoFocus`, used for the row at the old limit once its page arrives) and a visually hidden polite status says "7 more results, every match listed." or "20 more results." The control is never disabled while pending. Criterion "A reader goes on from the new rows" in MM-410; the test checks the status text and `document.activeElement`.
  - *Exactly a page of matches offers "More results" that adds nothing, and the end is silent* (F2 medium, PD-3 low): **accepted, by removing the case.** The packs are asked for one more than the page; the offer shows only while there are more. PD-3's quiet caption is therefore not drawn: the end is always shown by the layout. Criterion "Exactly a page" and its test.
  - *No busy state between the press and the next page* (PD-2 medium, F3's loading half low): **deferred to a measurement.** The read is local; MM-51 measured 16 ms for a two-prefix search on a laptop, and the direction shows nothing for a local read under about 150 ms. The owner measures a second page on the phone with the 2026-10-19 packs (handoff Q2); a spinner in the slot is built only if perceptible. Not disabling the button while pending is kept, as the guard asked.
  - *No failure state for a page that fails to read* (F3 medium): **a proposed ticket, MM-411.** `useAsync` swallows errors and a failed first page already renders nothing (pre-existing); rare on local SQLite. The owner accepts, changes or declines it.
  - *"A new query starts over" has no test* (F4 low): **accepted.** A test presses More, shortens the query, and sees one page with the offer back; and one for a one-page search.
  - *Weight and kind of the control* (PD-1, F5): **kept as drawn**, the text kind. MM-410's Decisions now say the Ember word marks the continuation and the outline the exit, so a later reader does not "fix" the mismatch. A row would read as a food; two stacked secondaries would be one loudness for two unequal choices.
  - *The label's noun, "results" versus "foods"* (PD-4 low): **the owner's call** (handoff Q1); "More results" stays meanwhile.
  - *The rows' 16 dp padding inside the padded sheet* (PD-5, pre-existing hypothesis): **to the owner's Storybook look** (handoff Q3); not this change.
- **Reviewer requests, answered.** Guard to designer (one kind for both buttons): answered by PD-1, keep two kinds. Guard to react-capacitor (a ref or autofocus on `MmListRow`): done, an `autoFocus` prop, one home per control kept (the row's own button). Guard and designer to QA (second-page latency): the owner's phone measurement, Q2. Designer to react-capacitor (re-fetching `limit + 1` rather than an offset): kept; the catalog sorts the whole hit set and slices, so an offset would re-fetch as much, and the rows keep stable keys.
- **Targeted challenges.** None run: the two reports agree on every shared finding (F2/PD-3, F3/PD-2, F4/PD coverage note, F5/PD-1) and differ on nothing material.
- **Safety and privacy.** Neither reviewer found a concern; nothing new leaves the device.
- **Owner decisions.** Q1 the noun; Q2 the latency and the spinner; Q3 the padding; MM-411 proposed.
- **Edit owner:** the parent session (chair).
- **Verification.** `npx tsc --noEmit` clean; `npx vitest run test/food/searchMoreResults.test.tsx`: 4 passed; the 349 story walks passed before the F1/F2 edits; `mm check` on the final tree is recorded in the pull request. Not seen in the three palettes by anyone in this run; not on a phone.
