First launch

  1. The app opens and reads what is stored.
  2. A new installation sees the theme chooser (MM-184). An existing one never does.
  3. Welcome, then seven steps: biological sex, date of birth, measurements, your day, training, health check, your plan.
  4. A date of birth under 18 stops the flow (MM-13).
  5. Start lands on the Dashboard in calibration.

Opening the app later

  1. Under 18 by the stored date of birth: adults only.
  2. A health check due again: the repeated health check (MM-112).
  3. Back after a gap or a pause: welcome back (MM-147).
  4. A weekly check-in that changed targets: the explanation sheet, once (MM-24, MM-138).
  5. Otherwise the Dashboard. What Health Connect allows is read now and whenever the app returns to the front (MM-68).

Log a food

  1. Add food, from the button on Dashboard or Food, or the plus on a meal.
  2. The sheet opens. Then one of:
    • type the numbers (filled in);
    • search (results), then how much;
    • scan a barcode; an unknown product offers reading its label;
    • estimate a meal;
    • pick from saved foods and recipes.
  3. Log it. The entry appears under its meal on Food, and the hero counts it.
  4. Tapping the entry edits it; swiping it away removes it, with Undo.
  5. At the end of the day, mark it complete or partial. A very low complete day is asked about.

Weigh in

  1. Type today’s weight on Dashboard or Progress, and Save. Or let it come from Health Connect.
  2. The trend redraws. Note anything that moves water as a weight event.
  3. Once a week, measure the waist.

Log a workout

  1. Start workout, or repeat the last one.
  2. Add the first exercise from the picker, or create your own.
  3. Per set: Start set (timed), Add set, then rest counts down to its target.
  4. The card says what a step up would be; the range it uses is yours to set.
  5. Finish workout. A best estimated max is named as a personal record. Times can be corrected by hand.

The weekly check-in

  1. The check-in runs by itself when the app opens on or after its day (MM-24). Nothing is asked.
  2. If targets changed, why is shown once, with the choice to keep last week’s.
  3. The Dashboard’s coach line and Coach carry it afterwards; every past change is in target history.
  4. Once a week Coach offers the recovery questions; two hard weeks lead to an offer to ease, never to a lower target.

Pause and return

  1. Settings → Pause: a reason, a start and a length.
  2. While paused, a banner says so and the hero counts against a guide.
  3. Resume from the pause screen; then one weigh-in restarts the coach.
