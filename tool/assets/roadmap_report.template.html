Where the roadmap and the tickets disagree

Completeness counts tickets by the status written in each ticket (done, in progress, proposed). It does not weigh effort: a small ticket and a large one count the same. "Done" means the ticket says done, not that it has been tried on a device or reviewed by a professional.
